# The Speech Gate decides whether to decode, never which words to keep

ADR 0001 keeps word-level judgement out of the Transcription Engine and records that an energy threshold, tried as a filter on recognized text, deleted quiet speech. Iteration 3 then showed the other side of that rule: while the user is silent, Whisper decodes the near-empty buffer anyway and fills the Pending Text with Recognizer Artifacts such as «Продолжение следует», and when speech resumes after a long pause the artifact can be confirmed ahead of the real words. Post-processing cannot help with a Live Transcript, and the no-speech probability the decoder reports proved unreliable in both directions on our probes. We therefore add a Speech Gate on the input side: the engine compares the audio it has not decoded yet with the noise of the same session and does not decode at all until speech is present; when it opens, decoding starts at the beginning of the speech, and on stop the trailing silence is not decoded either, which removes the «Thank you» tail by construction. This does not reverse ADR 0001: the gate never sees text and never chooses between words, it only chooses whether the decoder runs, and its thresholds are relative to the session rather than fixed. Because a lost word costs more than a stray artifact, the gate opens easily, keeps a margin of audio before the point where it opened, and closes only after clear silence; whatever still slips through remains Post-processing's job. The parameters are engine constants tuned on recorded fixtures with real microphone noise; a neural VAD stays in reserve for the day the relative-energy gate starts cutting quiet speech.
