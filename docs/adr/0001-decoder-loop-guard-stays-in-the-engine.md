# Decoder loop guard stays in the engine

The Transcription Engine is not allowed to judge words: Recognizer Artifacts such as «Продолжение следует» are left to Post-processing, because every word-level filter we tried (a phrase blacklist, an energy threshold) also deleted legitimate speech. Two failures are different in kind, though: a Decoder Loop, where Whisper repeats one n-gram on a near-empty buffer, and a trailing segment on stop whose no-speech probability is high. Both are decoder failures visible in the structure of the output, not in its vocabulary, and they surface on every session regardless of whether Post-processing is enabled or reachable. We therefore keep a structural guard inside the engine: a segment that repeats one n-gram three or more times is dropped, and the final tail on stop is dropped when the decoder itself reports no speech. The boundary is the shape of the output, never its words; anything that needs a dictionary, a language model or a judgement about meaning belongs to Post-processing.

_Amended by ADR 0002 (2026-09-07): the no-speech re-decode of the trailing segment on stop was removed. The Speech Gate now keeps the silence between the last word and the stop from being decoded at all, so the tail needs no verdict from the decoder; the Decoder Loop guard is unchanged._
