# A Quote Reply Block keeps the quote and the dictated comment together

Replying to a fragment of an agent response by dictation has to make it obvious, in the Composer and in the sent message, which fragment the comment is about. The cheap design puts two independent units into the Composer, a Quoted Fragment and a Dictation Block, and relies on them standing next to each other; but typed text can be inserted between them, one can be deleted without the other, and nothing in the sent message ties them together once they are apart. We therefore make the dictated reply one unit, the Quote Reply Block: the Quoted Fragment and the comment travel together, are removed together, are opened together in the Dictation Window where the fragment stands read-only above the transcript, and reach the agent as one quote followed by one comment. The price is a second kind of block next to the Dictation Block, with its own crease, tooltip and serialization, and the rule that only the comment can be resumed or edited. The typed variant of Quote Reply keeps the cheap design on purpose: a Quoted Fragment followed by text the user types, where adjacency is enough because the user watches their own words appear.
